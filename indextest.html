What is a TRON SR Candidate?

A TRON Super Representative (SR) Candidate is any account holder on the TRON network who applies to participate in governance. While anyone can apply, only the top 27 most-voted candidates become official Super Representatives responsible for producing blocks and securing the network. The next 100 runner-ups are designated as Super Representative Partners.

The Role They Play

Super Representatives form the backbone of TRON’s DPoS consensus mechanism:

  • Validating Transactions: Processing and packing transactions into new blocks.
  • Network Governance: Voting on parameters, upgrades, and fees.
  • Securing Blockchain: Maintaining 24/7 node uptime to keep the network decentralized.
  • Distributing Rewards: Sharing block rewards back with voting community members.

How to Stake & Vote

[Stake TRX] ➔ [Get Power] ➔ [Vote] ➔ [Earn Rewards]

Step 1: Stake (Freeze): Lock your TRX using a wallet like TronLink to earn Energy or Bandwidth alongside voting power.

Step 2: Receive TP: 1 staked TRX = 1 TRON Power (TP), giving you equivalent voting weight.

Step 3: Cast Votes: Navigate to the Vote section, find our SR address, and allocate your TRON Power.

Step 4: Claim Rewards: SRs automatically distribute shares. Manually claim accumulated rewards via your wallet anytime.
